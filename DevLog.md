After I got the lighting to work I also worked on adding a Camera that takes in mouse and keyboard inputs. The way that works is quite fascinating. Basically we assign a direction vector and a position for the camera. We adjust the direction by taking the cross product of a vector that basically points upwards and the camera direction. With that we can discern what the orthogonal vector is for right and left movement that is also a unit vector. We modify the position of the camera using just that. After that we use yaw and pitch (euler angles that basically are for horizontal and vertical camera movement). With that we can use the pixel change and pass in an xOffset and yOffset and modify the camera direction accordingly. I attached the light source to the camera so it illuminates all sides of the cube. But later it may be wise to work on indirect lighting too.
